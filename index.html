Features

Everything an Indian food stall needs

Built for the chaos of a real kitchen — billing, splits, kitchen tickets, sales tracking and multi-shop. Nothing extra.

Lightning-fast billing

Tap items to build an order. Print receipt in seconds. Works with Bluetooth and WiFi thermal printers. Works offline — always.

One item, many sizes

Thums Up in 200ml, 500ml and 1 litre bottle? Chicken biryani as half plate or full plate? Samosa as single or plate of four? Add one item with all its options — your menu stays clean and short. Cashier taps the item and picks the size in one second.

Bill by voice in busy rush

Morning breakfast rush with 10 customers waiting? Hold the mic and say “masala dosa two, cutting chai three”. Slippy finds the items, shows you the match and waits for your tap before adding. No wrong bills. No missed orders. Just faster billing when the counter is packed.

Smart split billing

Tiffin counter and tea counter under one roof? Each gets its own separate receipt automatically. Idly, dosa and vada on one bill. Tea and coffee on another. Customer pays each counter separately without any confusion. Just set it once and forget.

Kitchen gets the order instantly

Cashier bills at the counter. Kitchen printer fires the order ticket automatically. Cook sees “Masala Dosa ×2, Vada ×3” and starts immediately. No shouting across the counter. No missed items. Every bill automatically sends to kitchen — no extra button needed.

Watch all your dhabas from your phone

Running a morning tiffin centre and an evening snacks stall? Open Slippy on your personal phone and see both counters live. Today's bills. Cash collected. UPI received. All from wherever you are. No extra device. No extra cost. Both plans include this free.

Your WhatsApp is your daily khata

Every shift settlement shared to WhatsApp in one tap. Monday morning Rs.4,200. Tuesday Rs.3,800. Wednesday Rs.5,100. Your WhatsApp chat becomes your permanent sales record. Open it any day, any month, any year. Tax time? Show your CA the WhatsApp history. No app needed. No accountant needed. It is all there.

Multi-shop support

Run up to 3 shops under one account. Each shop is independent — own menu, own cashier. Check all of them from one phone. One subscription covers all.

Works offline always

No internet? No problem. Bill all day without internet. Data saves on your device and syncs to cloud when you're back online. Your business never stops.

Any printer works

Bluetooth and WiFi thermal printers. Two printers at once — counter for receipt, kitchen for tickets. 58mm and 80mm paper. Shreyans, ATPOS, TVS, Xprinter, Rongta — all supported.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need internet to use Slippy?+
No. Slippy works completely offline for billing. You only need internet for the first setup and to sync your data. Your billing never stops even if internet is down.
What is split billing?+
Split billing lets your tiffin counter and tea counter each print their own receipt from one billing screen. Customer who orders idly dosa and tea gets two receipts — one from the tiffin side and one from the tea side. Your cashier just taps Print and Bill once. Set it up per category and Slippy handles the rest every single time automatically.
What is a kitchen order ticket?+
When your cashier bills a masala dosa and vada, a small ticket prints automatically at your kitchen printer showing exactly what to make. Cook sees the order immediately without waiting for the cashier to shout or walk over. Works for any items you mark as kitchen items. No extra button for the cashier to press.
What are item variants?+
Variants let you put all sizes of one item together. For example Thums Up with 200ml at Rs.20, 500ml at Rs.40 and 1 litre at Rs.60 all under one Thums Up entry in your menu. When cashier taps Thums Up a small popup shows the three sizes and they tap the right one. Works the same way for biryani half and full plate, coffee cutting or full, samosa single or plate of four. Your menu stays short and clean instead of having ten separate entries for the same item.
How does voice ordering work?+
During morning rush hold the mic button and say what the customer ordered. Say “masala dosa two” and Slippy will find it in your menu and show you the match before adding it. You tap to confirm — nothing is added without your tap. If it did not catch clearly just retry. Works completely offline. No internet needed. Useful when the counter is busy and typing is too slow.
Can I check my sales from my personal phone?+
Yes. Open Slippy on your personal phone and login with your credentials. You will see your counter sales in real time without using a billing slot. How many bills today. Cash collected. UPI received. If you have two or three counters you see all of them together. Both Basic and Pro plans include this at no extra cost.
Which printers work with Slippy?+
Any ESC/POS thermal printer. Popular options: Shreyans KP307, ATPOS AT402, TVS-E, Xprinter, Rongta. Both Bluetooth and WiFi printers work. You can connect two printers at once — one for receipts, one for the kitchen.
Can I use it for multiple shops?+
Yes. The Pro plan gives you 3 billing devices with 3 independent menus under one account. Check all your shops from your personal phone anytime.
How are settlement reports shared on WhatsApp?+
At the end of each shift, your cashier taps the Share button. The report opens in your phone's share menu — send to WhatsApp, save, or share via any app. Your WhatsApp becomes your permanent business ledger, far longer than the 7 days we keep on our servers.
What if my tablet breaks?+
No problem. Your menu and reports are backed up to our servers. Install Slippy on a new device, login, and everything is restored. Contact us and we'll help you switch devices in minutes.
What if I forget my PIN?+
Contact us on WhatsApp. We'll reset your PIN in minutes. Your data is always safe.
Do I pay Google for in-app purchases?+
No. Slippy subscriptions are managed directly with us via WhatsApp and UPI. No Google Play billing. No app store commission. You pay us directly.
What Android version do I need?+
Android 8.0 or higher. Works on any Android phone or tablet.
