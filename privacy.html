LEGAL · PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, handled simply.

We keep things small on purpose. Here's exactly what we collect, what we don't, and how we look after it — written in plain English.

Last updated · May 2026 · Request data deletion →

What we collect

The basics, nothing extra

Just enough to run your billing — no marketing trackers, no funnels.

  • ✓Business name and phone number
  • ✓Menu items and categories
  • ✓Daily sales totals and reports
  • ✓Device information for billing

What we don't collect

Off-limits, by design

We never touch any of this. Not for ads, not for analytics, not ever.

  • ✕Your customers' personal data
  • ✕Individual transaction details
  • ✕Payment information
  • ✕Location data

How we use your data

To run the app, that's it

Three reasons your data exists on our servers.

  • ✓To provide billing service
  • ✓To sync your menu and reports
  • ✓To send daily summary notifications

How we store it

Encrypted & hosted in India

Modern infrastructure, sensible retention windows.

  • ✓Encrypted on secure servers
  • ✓Hosted in India (Supabase)
  • ✓Sales snapshots kept 7 days
  • ✓Menu data kept while subscribed
